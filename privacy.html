Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

ViceFlash is a desktop publishing tool operated by an independent creator based in France, who is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: github.com/EitanGrn.

2. Data we process

  • TikTok account information obtained with your permission through TikTok Login Kit (scope user.info.basic): your open ID and display name, used to show which account content will be posted to.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload content you have approved (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • The videos, captions and settings you choose to publish, and the publish status returned by TikTok.

We do not collect data about other TikTok users, and we do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Why and on what basis

Data is processed only to provide the publishing feature you requested (performance of a service, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). It is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on the creator's own computer (Windows Data Protection API) and are never written in plain text, shared or sent anywhere other than TikTok's official API. Videos are sent directly to TikTok.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok, as needed to upload and publish your content, under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. Retention

Tokens are kept until they expire or until you revoke access; they are then deleted. Publishing records are kept for up to 12 months to track post status.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent at any time by revoking ViceFlash in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) or by contacting us. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
