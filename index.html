ViceFlash

Publish your own gaming shorts to TikTok, with you in control.

ViceFlash is a desktop tool used by an independent creator to publish short-form GTA VI gaming videos (news, commentary and gameplay highlights) to the creator's own TikTok account.

How it works

1. Sign in with TikTok. The creator connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit. ViceFlash displays the connected account name before every upload.

2. Send to TikTok as a draft. A finished short is sent to the creator's TikTok inbox. The creator opens TikTok, adds sound or edits, chooses who can see it and publishes it.

3. Or publish after review. ViceFlash shows a preview, the destination account, an editable caption, the privacy options provided by TikTok (no default), interaction settings, content disclosure and TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. Nothing is posted until the creator confirms.

Privacy first

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the creator's own computer. ViceFlash does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not sell data and does not run ads.
